### 🎒 The Lab Notebook

> *"I don't have a giant corporate research department or a 50-person team. Just a laptop, a terminal, way too much tea, and a bad habit of forgetting to sleep when there's an unsolved puzzle on my screen."*

Hey, I’m **Akshit**. 

At heart, I’m that nerdy kid who takes things apart to see how the clockwork moves. I get an absurd amount of joy from taking problems that usually require a room full of specialists—like real-time video inference, multi-agent reasoning, or high-throughput telemetry—and figuring out how to build a lean, lightning-fast solution solo over a weekend.

Mr. Stark once said you have to run before you can walk. My version is: **understand the physics from first principles, strip away the bloat, and build it so well it runs on hardware you found in a desk drawer.**

### 🕸️ How I Think & Build

```
┌──[ The Engineering Notebook ]
│
├── 01 // CURIOSITY FIRST, ALWAYS

│
├── 02 // SENSOR-TO-SCREEN AGILITY
│   I love connecting the physical to the digital: camera feeds, audio waves,
│   and live transit telemetries turned into fast, tactile, responsive interfaces.

│
└── 03 // BUILT FOR THE NEIGHBORHOOD
    I don't build tech just to admire the code. I build practical, resilient systems
    engineered to solve real human problems and stay standing when things break.
```

### 🔬 What I Spend My Hours On

I don't stick to one neat little lane—I like swinging across whatever layer the problem requires:

- **👁️ Vision & Edge Optics**  
  Real-time video ingestion (RTSP/WebRTC) under microsecond constraints. Multi-target tracking, low-latency edge inference via TensorRT/ONNX, biometric matching, and tamper-evident audit logging.

- **🤖 Autonomous Multimodal Systems**  
  Building software agents that can genuinely perceive: parsing multi-frame video, pulling live web evidence, reasoning spatially, and orchestrating complex tasks autonomously without constant supervision.

- **🎙️ Speech & Acoustic Modeling**  
  Audio DSP, generative voice pipelines, and low-latency acoustic models that preserve emotional cadence and personal vocal identity across regional dialects.

- **⚡ Fast Distributed Engines**  
  High-concurrency microservices, event streams (Redis Streams, Celery, Channels), and rock-solid relational architectures that hold up under unpredictable real-time telemetry spikes.

- **🎮 Native Runtimes & Kinetic Mechanics**  
  Tinkering with C++ bindings, Flutter/Dart mobile physics, and low-overhead client engines that maintain buttery-smooth 60+ FPS performance.
